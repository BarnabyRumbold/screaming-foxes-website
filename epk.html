Biography

Screaming Foxes is the solo project of Liverpool-based songwriter Barny Rumbold who began recording almost by accident, tracking a few acoustic songs while making music for a friend's video game, and finding the results honest enough to build on. Every part, from writing to recording to production, has been done alone, with one rule: anything that isn't honest gets thrown away.

"Playing Games" is the first single, mixed and mastered by Jon McMullen (Michael Kiwanuka, Wet Leg, Fionn Regan). It's the opening statement for a debut album about learning to be alone, Solitude, due in 2027; minimialist, atmospheric songs songs shaped by the same misty, evocative imagery that runs through the project's artwork.
